<summary><b>🤖 My Robotics Philosophy & Approach</b></summary>

```
"A robot is not just a machine — it's a physical manifestation of human intelligence.
 Every sensor is a sense. Every actuator is a muscle. Every algorithm is a thought."
```

My approach to robotics follows a **systems thinking** methodology:

- **Perception First** — You can't act on what you can't sense.
- **Model Everything** — Good mathematical models = predictable behavior.
- **Fail Gracefully** — A robot that fails safely is infinitely better than one that doesn't fail at all.
- **Hardware-Software Co-design** — The best robotics engineers speak both languages.
- **Iterate in Simulation** — Test 100 times in Gazebo, deploy once in the real world.
